## Evidence

Obake can leave EMF Level 5, Ultraviolet, Ghost Orbs. On reduced-evidence difficulties, Ultraviolet is guaranteed to remain.

## Tests that separate it

**Confirm the evidence set.** Use the standard tools to verify each of EMF Level 5, Ultraviolet, Ghost Orbs. Only a positive reading counts; a missing reading matters on normal difficulty.

**Watch for the ability.** Can blink into a different ghost model during a hunt and leaves unique six-fingered handprints.

**Use the weakness.** Fingerprints can fade faster than usual.

## Behavior

Can blink into a different ghost model during a hunt and leaves unique six-fingered handprints. Fingerprints can fade faster than usual.

## Speed and hunt threshold

Standard hunt speed of 1.7 m/s. The base hunt sanity threshold is 50%.

## Strategy

Treat the evidence set as the first filter, not the answer. Many ghosts share two or three evidence types, so confirm the full set before you commit. Once the evidence is locked in, use the ability and weakness above to separate the remaining candidates.

On reduced-evidence difficulties, keep the guaranteed evidence in view and use the [evidence tracker](/tools/evidence-tracker/) to apply the difficulty rules automatically.

## Evidence

Dayan can leave EMF Level 5, Ghost Orbs, Spirit Box.

## Tests that separate it

**Confirm the evidence set.** Use the standard tools to verify each of EMF Level 5, Ghost Orbs, Spirit Box. Only a positive reading counts; a missing reading matters on normal difficulty.

**Watch for the ability.** Hunts sooner and moves faster while a nearby player is moving.

**Use the weakness.** Hunts later and moves slower while nearby players stand still.

## Behavior

Hunts sooner and moves faster while a nearby player is moving. Hunts later and moves slower while nearby players stand still.

## Speed and hunt threshold

1.2 m/s and a 45% hunt threshold while the closest player is still; 2.25 m/s and 65% while that player walks.

## Strategy

Treat the evidence set as the first filter, not the answer. Many ghosts share two or three evidence types, so confirm the full set before you commit. Once the evidence is locked in, use the ability and weakness above to separate the remaining candidates.

On reduced-evidence difficulties, keep the guaranteed evidence in view and use the [evidence tracker](/tools/evidence-tracker/) to apply the difficulty rules automatically.

## Evidence

Shade can leave EMF Level 5, Ghost Writing, Freezing Temperatures.

## Tests that separate it

**Confirm the evidence set.** Use the standard tools to verify each of EMF Level 5, Ghost Writing, Freezing Temperatures. Only a positive reading counts; a missing reading matters on normal difficulty.

**Watch for the ability.** Less active at high sanity and more likely to produce mist or shadow-form events.

**Use the weakness.** Has a low hunt sanity threshold.

## Behavior

Less active at high sanity and more likely to produce mist or shadow-form events. Has a low hunt sanity threshold.

## Speed and hunt threshold

Standard hunt speed of 1.7 m/s. The base hunt sanity threshold is 35%.

## Strategy

Treat the evidence set as the first filter, not the answer. Many ghosts share two or three evidence types, so confirm the full set before you commit. Once the evidence is locked in, use the ability and weakness above to separate the remaining candidates.

On reduced-evidence difficulties, keep the guaranteed evidence in view and use the [evidence tracker](/tools/evidence-tracker/) to apply the difficulty rules automatically.

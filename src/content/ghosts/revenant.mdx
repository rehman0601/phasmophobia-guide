## Evidence

Revenant can leave Ghost Orbs, Ghost Writing, Freezing Temperatures.

## Tests that separate it

**Confirm the evidence set.** Use the standard tools to verify each of Ghost Orbs, Ghost Writing, Freezing Temperatures. Only a positive reading counts; a missing reading matters on normal difficulty.

**Watch for the ability.** Gains a large speed increase while chasing a visible player during a hunt.

**Use the weakness.** Moves slowly when it has no player to chase.

## Behavior

Gains a large speed increase while chasing a visible player during a hunt. Moves slowly when it has no player to chase.

## Speed and hunt threshold

About 1.0 m/s while roaming; roughly 3.0 m/s while chasing. The base hunt sanity threshold is 50%.

## Strategy

Treat the evidence set as the first filter, not the answer. Many ghosts share two or three evidence types, so confirm the full set before you commit. Once the evidence is locked in, use the ability and weakness above to separate the remaining candidates.

On reduced-evidence difficulties, keep the guaranteed evidence in view and use the [evidence tracker](/tools/evidence-tracker/) to apply the difficulty rules automatically.

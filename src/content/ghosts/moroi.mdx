## Evidence

Moroi can leave Spirit Box, Ghost Writing, Freezing Temperatures. On reduced-evidence difficulties, Spirit Box is guaranteed to remain.

## Tests that separate it

**Confirm the evidence set.** Use the standard tools to verify each of Spirit Box, Ghost Writing, Freezing Temperatures. Only a positive reading counts; a missing reading matters on normal difficulty.

**Watch for the ability.** Hunt speed scales with average team sanity and can curse players through the Spirit Box or parabolic microphone.

**Use the weakness.** Stays disoriented longer when smudged during a hunt.

## Behavior

Hunt speed scales with average team sanity and can curse players through the Spirit Box or parabolic microphone. Stays disoriented longer when smudged during a hunt.

## Speed and hunt threshold

1.5 m/s at high sanity, rising to 2.25 m/s at low sanity and higher with line of sight. The base hunt sanity threshold is 50%.

## Strategy

Treat the evidence set as the first filter, not the answer. Many ghosts share two or three evidence types, so confirm the full set before you commit. Once the evidence is locked in, use the ability and weakness above to separate the remaining candidates.

On reduced-evidence difficulties, keep the guaranteed evidence in view and use the [evidence tracker](/tools/evidence-tracker/) to apply the difficulty rules automatically.

## Evidence

Onryo can leave Spirit Box, Ghost Orbs, Freezing Temperatures.

## Tests that separate it

**Confirm the evidence set.** Use the standard tools to verify each of Spirit Box, Ghost Orbs, Freezing Temperatures. Only a positive reading counts; a missing reading matters on normal difficulty.

**Watch for the ability.** Can start a hunt at any sanity after blowing out a flame.

**Use the weakness.** Lit flames prevent it from hunting.

## Behavior

Can start a hunt at any sanity after blowing out a flame. Lit flames prevent it from hunting.

## Speed and hunt threshold

Standard hunt speed of 1.7 m/s. The base hunt sanity threshold is 50%.

## Strategy

Treat the evidence set as the first filter, not the answer. Many ghosts share two or three evidence types, so confirm the full set before you commit. Once the evidence is locked in, use the ability and weakness above to separate the remaining candidates.

On reduced-evidence difficulties, keep the guaranteed evidence in view and use the [evidence tracker](/tools/evidence-tracker/) to apply the difficulty rules automatically.

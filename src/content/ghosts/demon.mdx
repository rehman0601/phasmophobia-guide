## Evidence

Demon can leave Ultraviolet, Ghost Writing, Freezing Temperatures.

## Tests that separate it

**Confirm the evidence set.** Use the standard tools to verify each of Ultraviolet, Ghost Writing, Freezing Temperatures. Only a positive reading counts; a missing reading matters on normal difficulty.

**Watch for the ability.** Has a rare chance to start a hunt at any sanity, ignoring the normal threshold.

**Use the weakness.** Shorter hunt cooldown, and crucifixes protect a larger area around it.

## Behavior

Has a rare chance to start a hunt at any sanity, ignoring the normal threshold. Shorter hunt cooldown, and crucifixes protect a larger area around it.

## Speed and hunt threshold

Standard hunt speed of 1.7 m/s. The base hunt sanity threshold is 70%.

## Strategy

Treat the evidence set as the first filter, not the answer. Many ghosts share two or three evidence types, so confirm the full set before you commit. Once the evidence is locked in, use the ability and weakness above to separate the remaining candidates.

On reduced-evidence difficulties, keep the guaranteed evidence in view and use the [evidence tracker](/tools/evidence-tracker/) to apply the difficulty rules automatically.

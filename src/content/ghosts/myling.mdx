## Evidence

Myling can leave EMF Level 5, Ultraviolet, Ghost Writing.

## Tests that separate it

**Confirm the evidence set.** Use the standard tools to verify each of EMF Level 5, Ultraviolet, Ghost Writing. Only a positive reading counts; a missing reading matters on normal difficulty.

**Watch for the ability.** Has quieter footsteps and vocalizations during hunts.

**Use the weakness.** Produces paranormal sounds more often, giving away its position.

## Behavior

Has quieter footsteps and vocalizations during hunts. Produces paranormal sounds more often, giving away its position.

## Speed and hunt threshold

Standard hunt speed of 1.7 m/s. The base hunt sanity threshold is 50%.

## Strategy

Treat the evidence set as the first filter, not the answer. Many ghosts share two or three evidence types, so confirm the full set before you commit. Once the evidence is locked in, use the ability and weakness above to separate the remaining candidates.

On reduced-evidence difficulties, keep the guaranteed evidence in view and use the [evidence tracker](/tools/evidence-tracker/) to apply the difficulty rules automatically.

## Evidence

Yokai can leave Spirit Box, Ghost Orbs, D.O.T.S. Projector.

## Tests that separate it

**Confirm the evidence set.** Use the standard tools to verify each of Spirit Box, Ghost Orbs, D.O.T.S. Projector. Only a positive reading counts; a missing reading matters on normal difficulty.

**Watch for the ability.** Talking near it temporarily raises its hunt threshold and interaction rate.

**Use the weakness.** During a hunt it only hears voices and detects electronics at short range.

## Behavior

Talking near it temporarily raises its hunt threshold and interaction rate. During a hunt it only hears voices and detects electronics at short range.

## Speed and hunt threshold

1.7 m/s. Hunt threshold rises to 80% while players talk nearby. The base hunt sanity threshold is 50%.

## Strategy

Treat the evidence set as the first filter, not the answer. Many ghosts share two or three evidence types, so confirm the full set before you commit. Once the evidence is locked in, use the ability and weakness above to separate the remaining candidates.

On reduced-evidence difficulties, keep the guaranteed evidence in view and use the [evidence tracker](/tools/evidence-tracker/) to apply the difficulty rules automatically.

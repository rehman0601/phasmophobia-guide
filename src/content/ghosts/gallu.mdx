## Evidence

Gallu can leave EMF Level 5, Spirit Box, Ultraviolet.

## Tests that separate it

**Confirm the evidence set.** Use the standard tools to verify each of EMF Level 5, Spirit Box, Ultraviolet. Only a positive reading counts; a missing reading matters on normal difficulty.

**Watch for the ability.** Becomes more aggressive after players use protective equipment such as crucifixes or salt.

**Use the weakness.** Becomes less aggressive after a hunt.

## Behavior

Becomes more aggressive after players use protective equipment such as crucifixes or salt. Becomes less aggressive after a hunt.

## Speed and hunt threshold

1.7 m/s base, rising to about 1.955 m/s when aggressive and dropping to 1.36 m/s when calm. The base hunt sanity threshold is 50%.

## Strategy

Treat the evidence set as the first filter, not the answer. Many ghosts share two or three evidence types, so confirm the full set before you commit. Once the evidence is locked in, use the ability and weakness above to separate the remaining candidates.

On reduced-evidence difficulties, keep the guaranteed evidence in view and use the [evidence tracker](/tools/evidence-tracker/) to apply the difficulty rules automatically.

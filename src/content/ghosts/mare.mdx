## Evidence

Mare can leave Spirit Box, Ghost Orbs, Ghost Writing.

## Tests that separate it

**Confirm the evidence set.** Use the standard tools to verify each of Spirit Box, Ghost Orbs, Ghost Writing. Only a positive reading counts; a missing reading matters on normal difficulty.

**Watch for the ability.** Turns off lights and breaks bulbs more often, and hunts earlier in darkness.

**Use the weakness.** Hunts later in a lit room.

## Behavior

Turns off lights and breaks bulbs more often, and hunts earlier in darkness. Hunts later in a lit room.

## Speed and hunt threshold

1.7 m/s. Hunt threshold is 60% in darkness and 40% with the lights on. The base hunt sanity threshold is 60%.

## Strategy

Treat the evidence set as the first filter, not the answer. Many ghosts share two or three evidence types, so confirm the full set before you commit. Once the evidence is locked in, use the ability and weakness above to separate the remaining candidates.

On reduced-evidence difficulties, keep the guaranteed evidence in view and use the [evidence tracker](/tools/evidence-tracker/) to apply the difficulty rules automatically.

## Evidence

Hantu can leave Ultraviolet, Ghost Orbs, Freezing Temperatures. On reduced-evidence difficulties, Freezing Temperatures is guaranteed to remain.

## Tests that separate it

**Confirm the evidence set.** Use the standard tools to verify each of Ultraviolet, Ghost Orbs, Freezing Temperatures. Only a positive reading counts; a missing reading matters on normal difficulty.

**Watch for the ability.** Speeds up in cold rooms and can show frosty breath during a hunt when the fuse box is off.

**Use the weakness.** Slows down in warm rooms.

## Behavior

Speeds up in cold rooms and can show frosty breath during a hunt when the fuse box is off. Slows down in warm rooms.

## Speed and hunt threshold

Hunt speed scales with room temperature, from about 1.4 m/s in warm rooms to 2.7 m/s in freezing rooms. The base hunt sanity threshold is 50%.

## Strategy

Treat the evidence set as the first filter, not the answer. Many ghosts share two or three evidence types, so confirm the full set before you commit. Once the evidence is locked in, use the ability and weakness above to separate the remaining candidates.

On reduced-evidence difficulties, keep the guaranteed evidence in view and use the [evidence tracker](/tools/evidence-tracker/) to apply the difficulty rules automatically.

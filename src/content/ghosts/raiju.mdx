## Evidence

Raiju can leave EMF Level 5, Ghost Orbs, D.O.T.S. Projector.

## Tests that separate it

**Confirm the evidence set.** Use the standard tools to verify each of EMF Level 5, Ghost Orbs, D.O.T.S. Projector. Only a positive reading counts; a missing reading matters on normal difficulty.

**Watch for the ability.** Active electronics near it boost its speed during hunts.

**Use the weakness.** Disrupts electronics from farther away when manifesting.

## Behavior

Active electronics near it boost its speed during hunts. Disrupts electronics from farther away when manifesting.

## Speed and hunt threshold

1.7 m/s normally; about 2.5 m/s near active electronics. The base hunt sanity threshold is 50%.

## Strategy

Treat the evidence set as the first filter, not the answer. Many ghosts share two or three evidence types, so confirm the full set before you commit. Once the evidence is locked in, use the ability and weakness above to separate the remaining candidates.

On reduced-evidence difficulties, keep the guaranteed evidence in view and use the [evidence tracker](/tools/evidence-tracker/) to apply the difficulty rules automatically.

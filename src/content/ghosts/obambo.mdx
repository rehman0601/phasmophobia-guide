## Evidence

Obambo can leave Ultraviolet, Ghost Writing, D.O.T.S. Projector.

## Tests that separate it

**Confirm the evidence set.** Use the standard tools to verify each of Ultraviolet, Ghost Writing, D.O.T.S. Projector. Only a positive reading counts; a missing reading matters on normal difficulty.

**Watch for the ability.** In its aggressive state it hunts earlier and moves faster.

**Use the weakness.** In its calm state it hunts later and moves slower.

## Behavior

In its aggressive state it hunts earlier and moves faster. In its calm state it hunts later and moves slower.

## Speed and hunt threshold

1.445 m/s calm, 1.955 m/s aggressive. The base hunt sanity threshold is 50%.

## Strategy

Treat the evidence set as the first filter, not the answer. Many ghosts share two or three evidence types, so confirm the full set before you commit. Once the evidence is locked in, use the ability and weakness above to separate the remaining candidates.

On reduced-evidence difficulties, keep the guaranteed evidence in view and use the [evidence tracker](/tools/evidence-tracker/) to apply the difficulty rules automatically.

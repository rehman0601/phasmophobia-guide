## Evidence

Deogen can leave Spirit Box, Ghost Writing, D.O.T.S. Projector. On reduced-evidence difficulties, Spirit Box is guaranteed to remain.

## Tests that separate it

**Confirm the evidence set.** Use the standard tools to verify each of Spirit Box, Ghost Writing, D.O.T.S. Projector. Only a positive reading counts; a missing reading matters on normal difficulty.

**Watch for the ability.** Always knows where players are during a hunt and can give a unique Spirit Box response.

**Use the weakness.** Slows dramatically when it gets close to its target.

## Behavior

Always knows where players are during a hunt and can give a unique Spirit Box response. Slows dramatically when it gets close to its target.

## Speed and hunt threshold

3.0 m/s at distance, slowing to 0.4 m/s near its target. The base hunt sanity threshold is 40%.

## Strategy

Treat the evidence set as the first filter, not the answer. Many ghosts share two or three evidence types, so confirm the full set before you commit. Once the evidence is locked in, use the ability and weakness above to separate the remaining candidates.

On reduced-evidence difficulties, keep the guaranteed evidence in view and use the [evidence tracker](/tools/evidence-tracker/) to apply the difficulty rules automatically.

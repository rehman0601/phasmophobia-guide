## Evidence

Goryo can leave EMF Level 5, Ultraviolet, D.O.T.S. Projector. On reduced-evidence difficulties, D.O.T.S. Projector is guaranteed to remain.

## Tests that separate it

**Confirm the evidence set.** Use the standard tools to verify each of EMF Level 5, Ultraviolet, D.O.T.S. Projector. Only a positive reading counts; a missing reading matters on normal difficulty.

**Watch for the ability.** Usually only shows its D.O.T.S. silhouette through a video camera when no players are in the room.

**Use the weakness.** Less likely to wander far from its ghost room.

## Behavior

Usually only shows its D.O.T.S. silhouette through a video camera when no players are in the room. Less likely to wander far from its ghost room.

## Speed and hunt threshold

Standard hunt speed of 1.7 m/s. The base hunt sanity threshold is 50%.

## Strategy

Treat the evidence set as the first filter, not the answer. Many ghosts share two or three evidence types, so confirm the full set before you commit. Once the evidence is locked in, use the ability and weakness above to separate the remaining candidates.

On reduced-evidence difficulties, keep the guaranteed evidence in view and use the [evidence tracker](/tools/evidence-tracker/) to apply the difficulty rules automatically.

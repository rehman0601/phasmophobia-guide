## Evidence

The Mimic can leave Spirit Box, Ultraviolet, Freezing Temperatures. It also always shows Ghost Orbs, on top of its main evidence.

## Tests that separate it

**Confirm the evidence set.** Use the standard tools to verify each of Spirit Box, Ultraviolet, Freezing Temperatures. Only a positive reading counts; a missing reading matters on normal difficulty.

**Watch for the ability.** Copies the behavior of other ghost types for short periods.

**Use the weakness.** Always produces false Ghost Orbs.

## Behavior

Copies the behavior of other ghost types for short periods. Always produces false Ghost Orbs.

## Speed and hunt threshold

Standard hunt speed of 1.7 m/s, matching the ghost it is imitating. The base hunt sanity threshold is 50%.

## Strategy

Treat the evidence set as the first filter, not the answer. Many ghosts share two or three evidence types, so confirm the full set before you commit. Once the evidence is locked in, use the ability and weakness above to separate the remaining candidates.

On reduced-evidence difficulties, keep the guaranteed evidence in view and use the [evidence tracker](/tools/evidence-tracker/) to apply the difficulty rules automatically.

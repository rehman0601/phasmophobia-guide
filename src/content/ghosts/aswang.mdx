## Evidence

Aswang can leave Ghost Writing, Freezing Temperatures, D.O.T.S. Projector.

## Tests that separate it

**Confirm the evidence set.** Use the standard tools to verify each of Ghost Writing, Freezing Temperatures, D.O.T.S. Projector. Only a positive reading counts; a missing reading matters on normal difficulty.

**Watch for the ability.** Accelerates faster than any other ghost while it has line of sight during a hunt.

**Use the weakness.** Cannot kill a player who is hidden inside an official hiding spot.

## Behavior

Accelerates faster than any other ghost while it has line of sight during a hunt. Cannot kill a player who is hidden inside an official hiding spot.

## Speed and hunt threshold

Base speed 1.53 m/s. Line-of-sight acceleration carries it to 2.53 m/s after roughly nine seconds. The base hunt sanity threshold is 50%.

## Strategy

Treat the evidence set as the first filter, not the answer. Many ghosts share two or three evidence types, so confirm the full set before you commit. Once the evidence is locked in, use the ability and weakness above to separate the remaining candidates.

On reduced-evidence difficulties, keep the guaranteed evidence in view and use the [evidence tracker](/tools/evidence-tracker/) to apply the difficulty rules automatically.

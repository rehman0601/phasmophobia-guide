## Evidence

Yurei can leave Ghost Orbs, Freezing Temperatures, D.O.T.S. Projector.

## Tests that separate it

**Confirm the evidence set.** Use the standard tools to verify each of Ghost Orbs, Freezing Temperatures, D.O.T.S. Projector. Only a positive reading counts; a missing reading matters on normal difficulty.

**Watch for the ability.** Can randomly shut a door and drain sanity from nearby players.

**Use the weakness.** After smudging, it is temporarily confined to its room.

## Behavior

Can randomly shut a door and drain sanity from nearby players. After smudging, it is temporarily confined to its room.

## Speed and hunt threshold

Standard hunt speed of 1.7 m/s. The base hunt sanity threshold is 50%.

## Strategy

Treat the evidence set as the first filter, not the answer. Many ghosts share two or three evidence types, so confirm the full set before you commit. Once the evidence is locked in, use the ability and weakness above to separate the remaining candidates.

On reduced-evidence difficulties, keep the guaranteed evidence in view and use the [evidence tracker](/tools/evidence-tracker/) to apply the difficulty rules automatically.

## Evidence

Thaye can leave Ghost Orbs, Ghost Writing, D.O.T.S. Projector.

## Tests that separate it

**Confirm the evidence set.** Use the standard tools to verify each of Ghost Orbs, Ghost Writing, D.O.T.S. Projector. Only a positive reading counts; a missing reading matters on normal difficulty.

**Watch for the ability.** Very active when first encountered and ages as players stay near it.

**Use the weakness.** Grows quieter, slower, and less active as it ages.

## Behavior

Very active when first encountered and ages as players stay near it. Grows quieter, slower, and less active as it ages.

## Speed and hunt threshold

Starts at 2.75 m/s with a 75% hunt threshold; each age step lowers speed and threshold, reaching 1.0 m/s and 15%. The base hunt sanity threshold is 75%.

## Strategy

Treat the evidence set as the first filter, not the answer. Many ghosts share two or three evidence types, so confirm the full set before you commit. Once the evidence is locked in, use the ability and weakness above to separate the remaining candidates.

On reduced-evidence difficulties, keep the guaranteed evidence in view and use the [evidence tracker](/tools/evidence-tracker/) to apply the difficulty rules automatically.

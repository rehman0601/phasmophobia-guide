## Evidence

Phantom can leave Spirit Box, Ultraviolet, D.O.T.S. Projector.

## Tests that separate it

**Confirm the evidence set.** Use the standard tools to verify each of Spirit Box, Ultraviolet, D.O.T.S. Projector. Only a positive reading counts; a missing reading matters on normal difficulty.

**Watch for the ability.** Roams toward a random player and drains more sanity when seen during manifestations and hunts.

**Use the weakness.** Taking a photo or video of the Phantom makes it temporarily invisible.

## Behavior

Roams toward a random player and drains more sanity when seen during manifestations and hunts. Taking a photo or video of the Phantom makes it temporarily invisible.

## Speed and hunt threshold

Standard hunt speed of 1.7 m/s. The base hunt sanity threshold is 50%.

## Strategy

Treat the evidence set as the first filter, not the answer. Many ghosts share two or three evidence types, so confirm the full set before you commit. Once the evidence is locked in, use the ability and weakness above to separate the remaining candidates.

On reduced-evidence difficulties, keep the guaranteed evidence in view and use the [evidence tracker](/tools/evidence-tracker/) to apply the difficulty rules automatically.

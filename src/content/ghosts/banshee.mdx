## Evidence

Banshee can leave D.O.T.S. Projector, Ghost Orbs, Ultraviolet.

## Tests that separate it

**Confirm the evidence set.** Use the standard tools to verify each of D.O.T.S. Projector, Ghost Orbs, Ultraviolet. Only a positive reading counts; a missing reading matters on normal difficulty.

**Watch for the ability.** Fixates on one player for the contract and can start hunts from its D.O.T.S. location; sings more often.

**Use the weakness.** Cannot kill anyone except its target while the target is inside the investigation area.

## Behavior

Fixates on one player for the contract and can start hunts from its D.O.T.S. location; sings more often. Cannot kill anyone except its target while the target is inside the investigation area.

## Speed and hunt threshold

Standard hunt speed of 1.7 m/s. The base hunt sanity threshold is 50%.

## Strategy

Treat the evidence set as the first filter, not the answer. Many ghosts share two or three evidence types, so confirm the full set before you commit. Once the evidence is locked in, use the ability and weakness above to separate the remaining candidates.

On reduced-evidence difficulties, keep the guaranteed evidence in view and use the [evidence tracker](/tools/evidence-tracker/) to apply the difficulty rules automatically.

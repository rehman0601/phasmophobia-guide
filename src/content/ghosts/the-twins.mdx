## Evidence

The Twins can leave EMF Level 5, Spirit Box, Freezing Temperatures.

## Tests that separate it

**Confirm the evidence set.** Use the standard tools to verify each of EMF Level 5, Spirit Box, Freezing Temperatures. Only a positive reading counts; a missing reading matters on normal difficulty.

**Watch for the ability.** Can hunt from a different location than expected; the two twins have different speeds.

**Use the weakness.** Can interact with the environment in two places at once.

## Behavior

Can hunt from a different location than expected; the two twins have different speeds. Can interact with the environment in two places at once.

## Speed and hunt threshold

The main twin hunts at 1.7 m/s; the decoy is slower, with occasional faster hunts. The base hunt sanity threshold is 50%.

## Strategy

Treat the evidence set as the first filter, not the answer. Many ghosts share two or three evidence types, so confirm the full set before you commit. Once the evidence is locked in, use the ability and weakness above to separate the remaining candidates.

On reduced-evidence difficulties, keep the guaranteed evidence in view and use the [evidence tracker](/tools/evidence-tracker/) to apply the difficulty rules automatically.

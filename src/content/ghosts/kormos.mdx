## Evidence

Kormos can leave Ghost Orbs, Spirit Box, Ultraviolet.

## Tests that separate it

**Confirm the evidence set.** Use the standard tools to verify each of Ghost Orbs, Spirit Box, Ultraviolet. Only a positive reading counts; a missing reading matters on normal difficulty.

**Watch for the ability.** Hunts earlier when nearby players sprint outside of hunts.

**Use the weakness.** Nearly blind: cannot see players who stand still or are far away.

## Behavior

Hunts earlier when nearby players sprint outside of hunts. Nearly blind: cannot see players who stand still or are far away.

## Speed and hunt threshold

1.7 m/s base, multiplying to about 3.65 m/s when it detects a sprinting player. The base hunt sanity threshold is 50%.

## Strategy

Treat the evidence set as the first filter, not the answer. Many ghosts share two or three evidence types, so confirm the full set before you commit. Once the evidence is locked in, use the ability and weakness above to separate the remaining candidates.

On reduced-evidence difficulties, keep the guaranteed evidence in view and use the [evidence tracker](/tools/evidence-tracker/) to apply the difficulty rules automatically.

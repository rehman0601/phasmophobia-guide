## Evidence

Jinn can leave EMF Level 5, Ultraviolet, Freezing Temperatures.

## Tests that separate it

**Confirm the evidence set.** Use the standard tools to verify each of EMF Level 5, Ultraviolet, Freezing Temperatures. Only a positive reading counts; a missing reading matters on normal difficulty.

**Watch for the ability.** Moves faster when chasing a player from far away and can drain nearby sanity.

**Use the weakness.** Cannot use its abilities while the fuse box is off.

## Behavior

Moves faster when chasing a player from far away and can drain nearby sanity. Cannot use its abilities while the fuse box is off.

## Speed and hunt threshold

1.7 m/s normally; 2.5 m/s while far from its target with the breaker on. The base hunt sanity threshold is 50%.

## Strategy

Treat the evidence set as the first filter, not the answer. Many ghosts share two or three evidence types, so confirm the full set before you commit. Once the evidence is locked in, use the ability and weakness above to separate the remaining candidates.

On reduced-evidence difficulties, keep the guaranteed evidence in view and use the [evidence tracker](/tools/evidence-tracker/) to apply the difficulty rules automatically.

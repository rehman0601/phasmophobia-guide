## Evidence

Deildegast can leave EMF Level 5, Ghost Writing, D.O.T.S. Projector.

## Tests that separate it

**Confirm the evidence set.** Use the standard tools to verify each of EMF Level 5, Ghost Writing, D.O.T.S. Projector. Only a positive reading counts; a missing reading matters on normal difficulty.

**Watch for the ability.** Begins the contract at higher speed and slows down each time players move or interact with items.

**Use the weakness.** Its speed resets after every hunt, so item interactions between hunts expose it.

## Behavior

Begins the contract at higher speed and slows down each time players move or interact with items. Its speed resets after every hunt, so item interactions between hunts expose it.

## Speed and hunt threshold

Starts at 1.7 m/s and can drop to 0.4 m/s as items are disturbed; speed resets after each hunt. The base hunt sanity threshold is 50%.

## Strategy

Treat the evidence set as the first filter, not the answer. Many ghosts share two or three evidence types, so confirm the full set before you commit. Once the evidence is locked in, use the ability and weakness above to separate the remaining candidates.

On reduced-evidence difficulties, keep the guaranteed evidence in view and use the [evidence tracker](/tools/evidence-tracker/) to apply the difficulty rules automatically.

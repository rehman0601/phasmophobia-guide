See glowing orbs floating in the ghost's favorite room on video.

## How to Test

- Place a Video Camera in the ghost room and check the Truck monitor.
- Use Head Gear with night vision for portable orb detection.
- Orbs move with the ghost if the favorite room changes.

## False Positives

- Dust or insects can look like orbs — look for floating, glowing movement patterns.
- Orbs only appear in the ghost room.

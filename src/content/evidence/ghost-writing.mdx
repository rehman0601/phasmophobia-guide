# Ghost Writing

Find writing left in a Ghost Writing Book placed in a room.

## How to Test

- Place Ghost Writing Books in multiple rooms.
- Check books periodically for writing appearing on pages.
- Writing can appear even when no one is in the room.

## False Positives

- Writing may take time to appear — be patient.
- Some ghosts write more frequently than others.

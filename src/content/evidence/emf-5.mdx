Detect electromagnetic energy spikes. Only a reading of 5 counts as evidence; levels 2–4 come from every ghost.

## How to Test

- Use the EMF Reader in the ghost room or near the ghost.
- Watch for the red light and sound cue when the reader hits level 5.
- Levels 2–4 are not evidence — only 5 counts.

## False Positives

- EMF readings of 2–4 occur with any ghost and are not evidence.
- Electronics malfunction during hunts can spike readings.

# Spirit Box

Ask questions and listen for ghost responses through the Spirit Box.

## How to Test

- Turn off the lights in the room (optional but helps).
- Hold the Spirit Box and ask questions like "Are you here?" or "Show yourself."
- Listen for a voice response through the device.

## False Positives

- The ghost may not respond if multiple people are nearby.
- Silence is not evidence of absence — try again or try alone.

## Guaranteed Evidence
Guaranteed visible for: Deogen, Moroi.

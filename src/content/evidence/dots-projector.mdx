See the ghost's silhouette as it walks through the D.O.T.S. grid.

## How to Test

- Place the D.O.T.S. Projector where the ghost is active.
- Watch through the projector grid or a Video Camera for a human silhouette walking.
- Ghost turns walks into a 6-second silhouette.

## False Positives

- Some ghosts (Phantom, Oni, Deogen, Shade, Goryo) don't show silhouettes through D.O.T.S. even if it's their evidence.
- Silhouette must be a full human shape walking through the grid.

## Guaranteed Evidence
Guaranteed visible for: Goryo.

# Freezing Temperatures

Detect temperatures below 0°C (32°F) with a Thermometer.

## How to Test

- Use the Thermometer in various rooms to find cold spots.
- Watch your breath — visible breath appears at 5°C or colder, but only below 0°C counts as evidence.
- The ghost room is typically the coldest.

## False Positives

- Any ghost cools its current room to 1°C — this is not freezing.
- Visible breath starts at 5°C but is not evidence.

Guaranteed visible for: Hantu.

# Ultraviolet

Reveal fingerprints, handprints, or footprints left by the ghost with a UV Light.

## How to Test

- Turn off lights for best visibility.
- Shine the UV Light on doors, light switches, windows, and objects the ghost touched.
- Look for glowing fingerprints or handprints.

## False Positives

- Obake may leave six-fingered handprints - still counts as Ultraviolet evidence.
- Some surfaces don't show prints clearly.

## Guaranteed Evidence
Guaranteed visible for: Obake.
